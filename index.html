Oxford University Colleges — Visitor Guide

Visiting Colleges

Oxford has 39 colleges. Most charge £5-10 entry for visitors. Some require advance booking. Opening times vary — always check ahead.

Christ Church — skip this trip

The most famous college. Harry Potter staircase, cathedral, meadow. Nice but doesn't fit this trip — expensive (£18-24), busy, timed entry, and the free museums + walking tour cover Oxford brilliantly.

  • Entry: ~£18-24 (adult) — includes multimedia guide, cathedral + meadow
  • Book: Online at chch.ox.ac.uk — REQUIRED in summer. Tickets released each Friday.
  • Great Hall: Closed 12:00-14:00 for student lunches — plan around this
  • Allow: 1-1.5 hours
  • Highlight: The Great Hall (dining hall), Tom Tower, Christ Church Cathedral
Christ Church on maps

Magdalen College

Most beautiful college grounds. Deer park, riverside walks, stunning chapel. Froggy will love the gardens.

  • Entry: ~£8 (adult)
  • Open: Daily 12:00-18:00 (July, check ahead)
  • Allow: 1-1.5 hours
  • Highlight: Deer park, Great Quad, riverside walk, chapel
Magdalen College on maps

New College

Harry Potter cloisters. Beautiful medieval cloisters used in the films. Atmospheric gardens.

  • Entry: ~£5 (adult)
  • Open: Daily, check times
  • Allow: 30-45 min
  • Highlight: Cloisters (Chapel Cloister & War Memorial Garden)
New College on maps

Merton College

Oldest library in use. The Mob Library (1373) is extraordinary — still used by students today. Intimate, rarely crowded.

  • Entry: ~£5 (adult)
  • Open: Daily, check times
  • Allow: 30-45 min
  • Highlight: The Mob Library, Fellows' Quad
Merton College on maps

Practical Tips

  • Booking: Pay on the gate for colleges — no pre-booking needed (except Christ Church, which we're skipping).
  • Exam season: Colleges may close or limit access late May – mid June.
  • Photography: Usually allowed outside. Some colleges restrict inside photography.
  • Etiquette: Quiet voices. Don't sit on the grass (unless it's a designated area). Follow signs.
  • Tours: Some colleges offer guided tours at set times — ask at the porter's lodge.
  • Bodleian Library tour: Only 30-min Duke Humfrey's tours on Fri 31 Jul (~£12.50, buy at Weston Library from 9AM). Interesting but not essential — don't worry if you miss it.

Recipes

Cafetière Coffee

Froggy is bringing the cafetière. You just need ground coffee, milk, and sugar.

1Put 1 heaped spoon of ground coffee in the cafetière.
2Fill the kettle with water and bring to the boil. While you wait, take the milk out of the fridge.
3Pour the hot water into the cafetière over the coffee.
4Stir gently with a spoon to mix.
5Press the plunger down slowly and steadily.
6Pour ¼ milk into your mug first.
7Pour the coffee from the cafetière into the mug. Mix. The colour should be brownish.
8Add 1 teaspoon of sugar. Stir. Enjoy! (Froggy usually has 2 coffees in the morning.)

Tea

1Put the tea bag in your mug.
2Fill the kettle with water and bring to the boil. While you wait, take the milk out of the fridge.
3Pour the boiling water over the tea bag. Let it brew 3-4 minutes.
4Remove the tea bag. Pour in ¼ milk.
5Add 1 teaspoon of sugar. Stir. Enjoy!
